How to read the archive

Dawn is the young heir, Day the reigning emperor, Dusk the senior adviser, and Darkness the customary end of a Cleon's life. Cleon I predates this system.

Dating: Explicit means a date is directly stated in the show; approximate means reconstructed from time jumps or context. The Cleon number is not a calendar year. The events are a researched selection, not a complete scene-by-scene index. Events are grouped by the Cleon contemporaneous with them, not necessarily their direct participation. Cleon XXVI has not been established on screen.

Episode references refer to the Apple TV television adaptation, not the novels. Corrections and additional sourced events are welcome.

Sources and verification: Apple TV episode guide · Episode-by-episode plot guide · Community chronology · Season 3 finale analysis. Dates marked approximate are reconstructions. This is the television continuity, not the books.
