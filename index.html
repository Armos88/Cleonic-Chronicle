How to read the archive

Dawn is the young heir, Day the reigning emperor, Dusk the senior adviser, and Darkness the customary end of a Cleon's life. Cleon I predates this system.

Dating: Explicit means a date is directly stated in the show; approximate means reconstructed from time jumps or context. The Cleon number is not a calendar year. The events are a curated selection, not a complete scene-by-scene index.

Episode references refer to the Apple TV television adaptation, not the novels. Corrections and additional sourced events are welcome.
